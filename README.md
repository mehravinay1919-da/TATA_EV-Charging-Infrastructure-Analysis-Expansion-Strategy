# Tata Power EV Charging Infrastructure Analysis & Expansion Strategy

## Project Overview

This business analytics project explores EV charging demand, station utilisation, customer behaviour, revenue performance, and infrastructure investment to identify potential expansion priorities.

The project uses Microsoft Excel and its analytical tools to evaluate operational performance and develop data-driven recommendations for EV charging infrastructure planning.

## Business Problem

As electric vehicle adoption grows, charging network operators need to assess whether existing infrastructure can support future demand while maintaining operational efficiency and profitability.

This project examines charging patterns, customer segments, infrastructure gaps, and project profitability to identify potential expansion opportunities.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Power Pivot
- DAX
- Pivot Tables and Charts

## Analytical Areas

### 1. Business & Usage Overview
Analyse charging sessions, energy consumption, revenue, active customers, and demand trends.

### 2. Station Utilisation & Infrastructure
Evaluate station activity, sessions per station, charging capacity, and infrastructure gaps across districts.

### 3. Customer & Demand Behaviour
Explore customer usage, revenue contribution, income segments, and charging behaviour by vehicle model.

### 4. Investment & Profitability
Assess capital expenditure (CAPEX), projected returns, payback periods, and potential expansion priorities.

## Key Business Insights

The project presentation identifies the following findings:

- Charging demand is increasing across districts.
- Existing infrastructure may be insufficient to meet projected demand.
- Equipment cost is a major contributor to capital investment.
- Higher-income customers contribute more revenue in the analysis.
- Jayanagar, HSR Layout, and Banashankari are identified as priority locations for expansion.

## Recommendations

- Prioritise locations with high demand and infrastructure gaps.
- Evaluate project returns alongside CAPEX before approving expansion.
- Improve utilisation at underperforming charging stations.
- Monitor customer behaviour, energy consumption, and profitability.
- Use demand forecasting to support future infrastructure planning.
